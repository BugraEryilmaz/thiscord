Interface: 16 kHz mono, 512-point STFT, periodic sqrt-Hann window, 256-sample
hop; one frame per call with explicit conv/tra/inter caches. Thiscord runs it
through native Rust tract-onnx 0.21.4. The model is embedded in native clients
and processed locally; no runtime download is performed.
