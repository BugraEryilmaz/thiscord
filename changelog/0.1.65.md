## Highlights

- **Chat no longer jumps while you scroll.** Images now keep their exact size while they load, so the messages you are reading stay in place instead of being pushed down when a picture appears. This also fixes a jump that came back with spoiler images.

## Fixed

- Spoiler images briefly shrank and then grew every time they scrolled into view, which moved the chat.
- The first time an image appeared, its preview could change height when it finished loading.
