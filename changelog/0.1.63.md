## Highlights

- **Huddles: split a voice channel into private groups.** Plan up to four huddles from the new Huddles button in the voice panel, then split the room so each huddle hears only itself, and bring everyone back with the same button. Nobody leaves the call, so switching is instant, and screens and cameras stay visible to everyone.
- **One shortcut to split and regroup.** Record a shortcut right in the Huddles panel or in your shortcut settings. On desktop it works while a game is focused, and short sounds tell everyone when the room splits, regroups or someone is moved.
- **Drag people between voice channels.** If you can move members, drag someone in the channel list onto another voice channel. They show as moving until they arrive.

## New

- Plan huddles by dragging people between lanes, or select someone and press 1 to 4 (0 for the main room).
- While split, the channel list groups people by huddle and marks the people you can't hear, and the voice panel shows who is in your huddle.
- The new Split voice into huddles permission controls who can plan and split a channel. Nobody has it by default.
- A voice participant's right-click menu offers Move to for each channel they could be moved to.
