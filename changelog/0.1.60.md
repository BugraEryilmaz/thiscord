## Highlights

- **See what's new before you update.** The update prompt, Settings → Updates and the Updates button on the sign-in screen now show the highlights of the new version, with every change one click away under Show all changes.
- **Release notes in your language.** What's new appears in English or Turkish, following the language you chose in Thiscord.

## Improved

- The update prompt keeps its buttons in view while you scroll through the changes, and the download progress bar uses your accent color.
