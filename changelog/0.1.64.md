## Highlights

- **Adding someone to a server now sends an invitation.** When you add a person by username, they get a server invitation under Friends → Requests and choose to accept or decline. Nobody is put into a server without agreeing to it.
- **Safer Google sign-in.** A Google sign-in now finishes only in the app or browser that started it, so a forwarded sign-in link is useless to anyone else. This version is required to sign in with Google on desktop.
- **Confirm identity with Google works again.** Confirming with Google before a sensitive change failed for everyone. It now accepts a fresh sign-in to the Google account linked to your Thiscord account.

## New

- Server invitations expire after 7 days, and a server can have only as many open invitations as it has free member slots.
- Attachments you send now count toward a storage limit of 2 GB per server and per conversation. Deleting messages frees the space.

## Improved

- When connecting Google or confirming with Google fails, the message says what went wrong instead of showing the sign-in error.
- When a microphone or speaker cannot be opened, the error names the step that failed, which makes voice problems easier to report.
- Connecting Google to an account requires a verified email address.
- Verification and password reset emails are limited to one a minute and five an hour per address.
- A password reset cancels a pending email change.

## Fixed

- Email verification links were sometimes used up by mail scanners before you could click them.
