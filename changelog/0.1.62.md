## Highlights

- **Group chats and group calls.** Start a group with up to seven friends from New group on the Friends page or in your direct messages. Everyone can chat and share files, and anyone can start a call, with cameras and screen sharing, that rings the others and lets them join or leave whenever they like.
- **Voice messages.** Click the microphone in the message box to record, or hold it and release to send. Voice messages work in servers, direct messages and groups, with a waveform you can scrub and 1×, 1.5× and 2× playback.
- **Calls survive server restarts and network drops.** When the server restarts for an update, you rejoin your call automatically with mute and deafen kept. Lost connections are repaired without leaving the call.
- **Role colours and nicknames.** Roles can have a colour that tints members' names, and you can set your own nickname in each server.

## New

- Hide text with `||spoiler||`, or mark images, videos, files and voice messages as spoilers before sending. They stay hidden until clicked.
- Click "edited" on a message to see its earlier versions.
- Roles have a Show members separately option that decides which roles get their own section in the member list.
- Moderators with the new Manage nicknames permission can change other members' nicknames.
- Any member can rename a group, owners can also remove members and transfer ownership, and the member panel shows who is online.
- After a server restart, a screen share or camera that was on can be turned back on with one click.

## Improved

- Coloured names are adjusted automatically so they stay readable in every theme.
- Mention suggestions also find members by their server nickname.

## Fixed

- Long calls through a relay no longer drop after about an hour.
