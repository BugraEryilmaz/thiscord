## Highlights

- **Change your username or email.** Settings → Profile → Account details now has Edit beside both. A new username applies right away, and a new email takes effect once you open the link we send to it, so your current address keeps working until then.
- **Change or remove a server's join password.** Server settings → Overview has a Join password card that shows whether a password is on and lets you set a new one or remove it. Invite links and current members aren't affected.
- **Add your own games.** Settings → App behavior lets you pick a running program as a game, or type its program name, and choose how it appears to friends.
- **Hide games you'd rather not share.** When Thiscord detects a game, you can hide it on that computer and show it again later.

## New

- While an email change is waiting, the account card shows the new address with Resend link and Cancel change.
- Your previous email address gets a notice when the change is confirmed.
- App behavior shows the game detected right now.
- The join password field has a show/hide button.

## Improved

- Added games can also give a recognized game a different name.
